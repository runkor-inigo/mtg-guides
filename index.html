What these probabilities measure

60-card deck, opening seven, no mulligans, no opposing interaction. By T2 you have seen eight cards on the play or nine on the draw.

The two direct lines require one of 13 immediate green sources and one of six actual dorks by T1, then Cradle + Cub + the named payoff by T2. They measure the specific land → dork → Cradle + Cub → payoff sequence. Speaker being cast is not a completed infinite combo or a kill.

These are exact probabilities for those events, computed by combinatorial enumeration of the final draw category and inclusion–exclusion over the required categories in the earlier cards. Ordinary “seen” probabilities use the hypergeometric distribution: P(at least one of K in n) = 1 − C(60−K,n)/C(60,n).

GSZ → Arbor, Once Upon a Time, Quirion routes, other mana sequences and London mulligans are excluded. These line probabilities therefore are not the total probability of a T2 play or win.

Curve uses printed mana value with X = 0 off the stack: GSZ is MV 1, OUAT is MV 2, Vibrance is MV 5. Alternative costs do not change mana value. Lands are excluded from the curve.

Heuristics · decisions before actions

The matchup row shows a proposed test plan for this exact 75; adjust to the opposing build and play/draw. These principles explain decisions; they explain the proposed IN/OUT counts.
SIDEBOARD

Preserve the engine

Keep enough mana and creatures to make Cradle work. Large swaps are deliberate transformations. Speaker belongs to the loop engine group. Start with the matchup row rather than applying a universal number of cuts.

CONTROL

Discard is part of the plan

Control plans use discard and Choke in different quantities. The adapted Control (vegecookies) row cuts all three Natural Orders, Vibrance and Atraxa for three Thoughtseize and two Choke. Check the row for its exact exchanges.

MANA

19 lands · 7 fetches · 2 Boseiju

Only one Wooded Foothills remains. Boseiju is immediate green mana, but not a Forest for Quirion. Balance playing it as a land against retaining its channel ability. The second copy is legendary; it does not give two permanent Boseiju mana sources.

MANA

No Savannah / Massacre exposure

The current list contains no Plains type. Avoid introducing one unnecessarily against Massacre. The current sideboard uses only green, black and hybrid green/black cards.

SEQUENCING

Count Cradle after paying the bounce

An earthbent Cradle counts itself and has haste. Earthbend does not untap it. When Symbiote returns Speaker, count the remaining creatures before tapping Cradle, then add Cub’s bonus.

COMBO

Play and draw have different hand budgets

For the documented T2 Sabertooth route: play needs Quirion already in hand, two Symbiotes and three discards (including the recovered Forest). Draw uses three Symbiotes and four discards. Do not treat a tutored card as free discard fodder.

COMBO

Speaker’s final ETB is the exception

During the infinite loop, replay Speaker and decline to discard. Only at the finish: leave its ETB on the stack, return Speaker with Sabertooth, then discard that same Speaker to find Hoof. Speaker’s tap ability is unavailable in these T2 lines.

COMBO

Hardcasting Hoof is not always lethal

The earlier Quirion exit can cast Hoof on T2 but leaves the dork and Cradle tapped. With only Hoof attacking, that exact line is a 12/12 trampler. The completed infinite-mana line repeats Hoof ETBs on creatures that stay in play, then untaps animated Cradle for lethal combat. A recast Hoof loses its earlier boosts.

THREATS

Hogaak and the grind package

Preserve Speaker access when boarding Hogaak unless the explicit row says otherwise. Hogaak uses convoke/delve and cannot be cast with mana. Assess exile removal and Karakas before committing; do not assume every UB list has the same answers.

INTERACTION

Snuff Out and Oracle

Snuff Out only targets nonblack creatures. Removing Oracle in response to its ETB can reduce devotion, but does not stop a zero-card-library Oracle win. Treat it as pressure on specific piles, not a universal answer.

LOCKS

Protect your lands with Marwyn

Marwyn gives our lands hexproof against opposing targeted effects, including Wasteland. It does not prevent Blood Moon or protect Marwyn herself from removal. Against land-heavy decks, GSZ for X=2 can find her.

RESILIENCE

Vibrance and mana denial

Vibrance is more valuable when the opponent attacks mana. Its green mode can find a land such as Boseiju; its red mode can answer a small creature. Check the mana actually spent, especially under Moon. Use the matchup row for the cut.

RESILIENCE

Sweepers change commitment

Check the row’s warning before adding a fourth or fifth creature. Massacre, Fury, Wrath of the Skies, Terminus, Verdict and Deluge require different play-arounds. Treat the list as a watchlist, not proof of the opponent’s exact 75.

GRAVEYARD

Different hate serves different jobs

Three Leylines supply opening-hand graveyard hate; Thoughtseize can cover transformation plans that bypass graveyards. The list has no instant-speed graveyard answer in its sideboard. Do not automatically remove the loop or add Hogaak across the whole category.

TESTING

Ideas are separate from the registered list

Underground Mortuary was discussed but not added: surveil helps selection, while entering tapped can delay dork T1 or Speaker T2. Caller of the Claw is also a candidate, not part of the current 75.

EVIDENCE

Prioritize experienced pilots

Weight specialists’ reasoning and your own matchup testing. Repeated copies of one successful list are not independent agreement. Keep the vegecookies plan as a distinct alternative; metagame share describes popularity, not matchup win rate.

    Hand requirements and sequencing notes
    • Start with seven cards, without a mulligan. T1 cards must be available on T1; other cards must arrive before use. Tutor targets must remain in the library.
    • The play route requires Quirion in hand. Tutoring for it consumes another search and discard. Without it, the three-Symbiote route requires a fourth discard unavailable from the eight cards seen.
    • Quirion returns Forest/Bayou, not a sacrificed fetchland. The recovered permanent supplies a discard; it is not an extra draw. Boseiju is not a Forest and cannot be returned this way.
    • Stay in the same T2 main phase. Earthbend does not untap Cradle. Speaker is in hand when Cradle taps after each bounce; do not count it. Cub’s extra green is included in all totals.
    • Symbiote can untap the animated Cradle. Speaker has summoning sickness: never use its tap ability in these T2 lines.

    A — Find Hoof: prepare T3

    Use the second Speaker search for Craterhoof Behemoth and keep it in hand. End T2 with five creatures and Hoof ready for T3.

    The remaining 2 mana is available only now; it does not carry into T3. On T3, even first land (1) + dork/Arbor (2 with Cub) + Cradle (6) gives 9 mana before using any untap abilities, enough to hardcast Hoof if the board survives.

    Two discards total. Hoof is tutored; it need not be drawn.

    B — Find another Cub: grow the board

    Use the second search for Badgermole Cub. Spend the remaining 2 mana to cast it and earthbend the first land.

    End T2 with seven creatures: dork, two Cubs, Speaker, Symbiote and both lands. The first land remains tapped: earthbend grants haste but does not untap it. No mana remains in this line.

    Two discards total. Maximum board development among these three exits; Hoof has not been tutored.

    C — Find Quirion: hardcast Hoof on T2

    Requires Hoof already in hand and a Forest to return. With the first land being a Forest, search for Quirion Ranger, cast it for G and keep G floating.

    Return the tapped Forest with Quirion to untap animated Cradle. Six creatures remain: dork, Cub, Cradle, Speaker, Symbiote and Quirion. Tap Cradle for 6 + Cub's 1 = 7; together with the floating G, you have 8 mana to hardcast Hoof.

    2 − 1 + 7 = 8 → Craterhoof in T2.

    Combat: This is not automatically lethal. With this exact sequence only Hoof can attack: the dork and Cradle are tapped, and the other creatures entered this turn. Hoof sees seven creatures and attacks as a 12/12 trampler.

    Two discards plus Hoof already in hand. Do not discard Hoof to either Speaker trigger.

    Hand budget

    The five setup cards are the first land, dork/GSZ, Cradle, Cub and Speaker. Add two distinct discardable cards: seven cards cover exits A and B. The searches replace each discarded card with the creature you need; returning and recasting Speaker does not supply discard fodder.

    Exit C additionally needs Hoof: eight cards total by T2. On the play, an opening seven plus the normal T2 draw can supply exactly that set; there is no spare card beyond the two discards. For example, open the five setup cards + Hoof + one discardable card, and discard the T2 draw as the second card. Required cards must arrive before their respective play/search.

    Speaker's tap ability is never used here. Symbiote can untap Cradle because it is a creature after earthbend. The five-mana activation happens while Speaker is in hand; count four creatures, not five.

    Sabertooth Loop

    Speaker loop · net +2 green per cycle

    Entry state: eight creatures, Speaker on the battlefield, Cradle tapped, Symbiotes used, and three green floating. This is the endpoint of either T2 route above.

    1. A · Reset one Symbiote

      Pay to Sabertooth to return a Symbiote. Recast it for . As a new object, it can activate again.

      0 × floatingFirst cycle: spend the initial 3 green

    2. B · Bounce Speaker → untap Cradle

      Activate the new Symbiote: return Speaker and untap Cradle. Seven creatures remain. Tap Cradle: 7 + Cub’s 1 = 8 green.

      8 × floatingSpeaker in hand

    3. C · Recast Speaker → repeat

      Cast Speaker . Resolve its ETB without discarding or searching. Eight creatures are back on the battlefield.

      5 × floatingSame entry state · 2 more green
    8 produced − 3 Speaker − 2 bounce − 1 Symbiote = +2 per cycle ↻

    Repeat a finite number of times large enough for the finish. No further discard fodder is needed.

    Finish · use the pending ETB only here

    1. 1 · Cast Speaker; keep its ETB on the stack

      After accumulating enough mana, respond to the ETB by paying to Sabertooth. Return Speaker to your hand.

    2. 2 · Discard that Speaker → find Hoof

      Resolve the pending ETB. Discard Speaker itself and find Craterhoof Behemoth. No additional card is required.

    3. 3 · Cast Hoof → repeat ETBs → attack

      Cast Hoof for . With enough mana, return it for and recast it repeatedly. Boosts accumulate on creatures that stay on the battlefield; each recast Hoof is a new object. Reset a Symbiote, return the dork to untap animated Cradle, then attack with the repeatedly boosted Cradle and hasty Hoof.

    Visionary variant · draw through the deck

    Badgermole Cub + Temur Sabertooth + Wirewood Symbiote + Elvish Visionary + earthbent untapped Gaea's Cradle

    Five creatures are in play including Cradle. Tap Cradle for 5, Cub adds 1 → 6 mana. Symbiote returns Visionary and untaps Cradle. Sabertooth returns Symbiote for 2, recast Symbiote for 1, recast Visionary for 2 and draw.

    Loop cost = 5 · output = 6 → +1 green mana +1 card per cycle
    Endurance turns the completed engine into a recursive endgame: recycle Boseiju / graveyard resources and avoid decking.
