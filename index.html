Core heuristics

Doomsday / Oracle

Keep Ouphe for LED/Petal. Cut Vibrance and the grind package. Snuff Out can force Thassa's Oracle piles to account for reduced blue devotion if Oracle dies before its trigger resolves.

Massacre / white-source rule

Do not expose a Plains type unnecessarily against UB decks with Massacre. The current build removes Savannah entirely. Teeg becomes primarily a GSZ bullet; a naturally drawn Teeg can be discarded to Speaker.

Hogaak package

If Hogaak comes in, keep all 3 Formidable Speakers by default. Speaker is part of the Hogaak package because it converts a low-value card into the tutorable 8/8 grind threat.

Vibrance / mana-lock rule

Default: Vibrance is a flex cut when the matchup does not attack our mana. Keep it against Wasteland. Also keep it when it has a specific land-lock job: versus Back to Basics it tutors Boseiju; versus Magus of the Moon, Magus itself can enable RR so Vibrance becomes a tutorable 3-damage answer.

Mass-critical principle

Against fair decks, 2–4 cards is the default. Five changes demand a reason. Six or more means we are deliberately transforming the deck.

Hogaak vs UB

Speaker can tutor Hogaak after the first wave of interaction. Current UB removal is poor at exiling or cleanly answering an 8/8 black/green threat. Do not bring Hogaak into Swords/Karakas shells.

Snuff Out

Tempo removal, not generic fair-deck removal. Best when zero mana lets us continue Cub/GSZ/NO development. Remember: nonblack target only.

Natural Order

Four by default. Go to three against hard tempo when duplicate NO is too costly. Do not cut it by reflex against mana-denial decks.

Visionary + Sabertooth loop

Keep in long games and against Tron/Lands. Cut when Bowmasters/hard tempo make a 4-mana loop package unrealistic.

Gaddock Teeg

Teeg is a Control-plan bullet, not a passive value creature. It shuts off our own GSZ/NO after resolving. Find it first, lock the opponent, then use Sabertooth if we need to unlock our own spells.

Sweepers

Always check the orange warning before committing the fourth/fifth creature. Massacre, Wrath of the Skies, Eldrazi Confluence, Fury, Brotherhood's End, Terminus, Supreme Verdict and Toxic Deluge-style effects all change sequencing.

Caller of the Claw

Caller is not in the current 75, but its relevance is tracked matchup-by-matchup. If sweeper density remains high in real testing, Caller becomes a serious candidate because Speaker/OUAT can access it.

Thoughtseize compression

The main reason to stay BG: the same four slots cover Reanimator, Show, Aluren, Doomsday, TES and Tron. Do not side it in just because the opponent is blue or black.

Leyline + Endurance split

Leyline gives turn-zero power while Endurance remains a creature/engine card and a usable topdeck. Thoughtseize covers Show-and-Tell transformation plans that bypass graveyard hate.

Sabertooth / Symbiote / Visionary loop

Badgermole Cub + Temur Sabertooth + Wirewood Symbiote + Elvish Visionary + earthbent untapped Gaea's Cradle

Five creatures are in play including Cradle. Tap Cradle for 5, Cub adds 1 → 6 mana. Symbiote returns Visionary and untaps Cradle. Sabertooth returns Symbiote for 2, recast Symbiote for 1, recast Visionary for 2 and draw.

Loop cost = 5 · output = 6 → +1 green mana +1 card per cycle
Endurance turns the completed engine into a recursive endgame: recycle Boseiju / graveyard resources and avoid decking.
